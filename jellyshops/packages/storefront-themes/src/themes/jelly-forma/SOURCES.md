# Photography

## Refined starter and preview

The `*-v2.webp` assets were generated with OpenAI Image Gen on 7 November 2026 for this independent theme. They are illustrative furniture images, not photographs of merchant inventory. The set includes a landscape sofa scene, eight isolated category objects and two studio views of the sample oak stool. Generated PNG originals were resized and encoded as WebP with Sharp; transparency was preserved for the category cutouts. Replace them with actual catalogue photography before using them to represent products for sale.

No reference-store photography, copy or branding was copied. The existing `dining-room.jpg` remains in the editable image-with-text section; the other original photographs remain available in the package.

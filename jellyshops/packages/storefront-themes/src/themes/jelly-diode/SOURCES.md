# Images

The ten photographs in `assets/*.webp` were generated with ChatGPT image generation on 9 November 2026 for this independent theme (one conversation, prompts following the image list in `docs/theme-briefs/jelly-diode.md`): hero headphones on graphite, six product shots on light grey (headphones, earbuds in a case, compact mechanical keyboard, fabric speaker, USB-C charger with cable, power bank), a desk set-up, a keyboard switch close-up and an "in the box" flat lay. All prompts asked for no text, logos or brand names. PNG originals (1254-1536 px) were resized and encoded as WebP (quality 80) with Sharp.

They are illustrative images, not photographs of merchant inventory. Replace them with real product photography before selling.

`mark.svg` and `preview.svg` are original vector artwork drawn by `scripts/build-diode-theme.mjs`.

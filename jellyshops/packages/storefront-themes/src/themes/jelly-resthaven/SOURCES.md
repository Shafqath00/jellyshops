# Images

The nine photographs in `assets/*.webp` were generated with ChatGPT image generation on 11 November 2026 for this independent theme, in one conversation: a home banner bedroom (`hero`), three room tiles for the large menu (`room-main`, `room-guest` with an ottoman bed lifted to show its storage, `room-kids`), a divan with a drawer open (`divan`), a cut-away hybrid mattress (`mattress`), a channel-stitched velvet headboard (`headboard`), a flat lay of upholstery fabric swatches (`fabrics`) and an upholstery detail used as a second photo (`detail`). Every prompt asked for realistic interior or product photography with no text, logos, brand names or people. PNG originals (1536 x 1024) were encoded as WebP (quality 80) with Sharp.

`bedroom.png` is the theme's earlier photograph; it is kept because stores that installed Resthaven before 11 November 2026 still use it.

They are illustrative images, not photographs of merchant inventory. Replace them with real product photography before selling.

`mark.svg` and `preview.svg` are original vector artwork.

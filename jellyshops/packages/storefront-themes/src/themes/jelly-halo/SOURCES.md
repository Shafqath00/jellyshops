# Images

The ten photographs in `assets/*.webp` were generated with ChatGPT image generation on 8 November 2026 for this independent theme (one conversation, prompts following the image list in `docs/theme-briefs/jelly-halo.md`): hero serum bottle on blush, six product shots on warm off-white (serum, cream jar, cleanser tube, SPF tube, facial oil, lip balm), a cream and serum texture close-up, a bathroom-shelf routine scene and a citrus ingredient still life. All prompts asked for no text, logos or brand names. PNG originals (1024-1536 px) were resized and encoded as WebP (quality 80) with Sharp.

They are illustrative images, not photographs of merchant inventory. Replace them with real product photography before selling.

`mark.svg` and `preview.svg` are original vector artwork drawn by `scripts/build-halo-theme.mjs`.

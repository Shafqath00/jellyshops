# Jelly Shop marketing assets (black-and-white direction)

All images were generated with ChatGPT (image generation) in the owner's signed-in Chrome on 2026-11-15, one fresh chat per image so earlier images don't bleed into later ones. Originals (PNG, 1.5–2.2 MB) are in the owner's Downloads folder as `js3-*.png`; the site uses resized WebP copies in `jellyshops/public/assets/` (OG image as JPEG for social sites). Palette: strictly black, white and greys (`#0A0A0A` background), per `DESIGN_TOKENS.md`. Third-party logos are not images we generated; see `LOGOS.md`.

## Shared style lines

**`S3` (product visuals):**
> Premium dark 3D render, strictly black, white and grey only, absolutely no colour. Matte black and graphite objects, frosted glass panels, crisp white UI with short readable text, soft white rim light and a gentle white glow, on a near-black #0A0A0A background with a faint fine grid of thin grey lines that fades out at the edges. Minimal, expensive, spacious, high contrast. No people, no real brand logos. Prices in British pounds (£).

**`T3` (template previews):**
> Create one landscape image, 1536x1024. Premium dark render, strictly black-and-white monochrome, absolutely no colour (every photo inside is black-and-white too): one large floating desktop browser window with a thin graphite frame and a soft white rim light, tilted very slightly, on a near-black #0A0A0A background with a faint fine grid fading at the edges and a soft white glow behind the window.
